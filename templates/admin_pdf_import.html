Welcome to PDF Batch Import. Step 1: choose your task, PDF(s) and optional page range, then click "Load PDF". Step 2: click "Detect questions" (and "Detect parts" for structured papers), or draw boxes yourself.
Defining bounding boxes
Auto: pages with a printed frame (dashed green guide) take its edges; other pages share the widest box's width.
Parts

Advanced: detection method, custom prompt, debug
Refine: model boxes snapped to the printed content by computer vision. Segment: the model only marks where each question starts; computer vision derives the boxes (single column only). Both need NumPy; each page can be re-run with another method.
Experimental Full auto: reads the whole paper, locates and splits every question, checks its own boxes and lists what needs a look. Uses more model calls and replaces the boxes in this session.
How to edit boxes
  • Drag a box to move it, drag its handles to resize, select it and press Delete to remove it. After clicking a box, the arrow keys nudge it (Shift = 10× faster); hold H while dragging to hide the magnifier. Click a crop preview to enlarge it.
  • Add box ▾ on a page: Add question takes the next number and shifts later questions up where they would clash; Add part takes the next letter of the question it is drawn in (the first part trims the question box down to its stem); Add stem adds the shared background of the question above.
  • Deleting the last box of a question shifts later question numbers down. Typing a Q# and moving / resizing never renumber anything. Renumber from here on a page makes that page and every later page sequential (parts follow their question).
  • Q# can be 3, a part 3a, or a shared range 23-24. Roles come from the labels: typing 4d and 4di makes 4d a nested stem. The + button on a row adds the next part beneath it.
  • Per question: Re-split / Unsplit. Per page: Re-run (choose method / endpoint from its menu), Re-split page, Frame.
  • QUE SOL stem part — then use Step 3 to import.
